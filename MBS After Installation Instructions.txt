Thank you for installing Marble Blast Super.

Please copy & paste the following files to the MBS directory.
(If you do not have some of them in MBG, DO NOT worry about them.)
(You may either have the non-ignition version OR the ignition version, so some of the files below will not appear.):

================================

To the folder
.\Marble Blast Super :

NON-IGNITION VERSION:

glu2d3d.dll
main.cs
marbleblast.exe
ogg.dll
openal32.dll
opengl2d3d.dll
vorbis.dll

IGNITION VERSION:

glu2d3d.dll
ignition
main.cs
MarbleBlastGold.exe
ogg.dll
OpenAL32.dll
opengl2d3d.dll
vorbis.dll

================================

To the folder
.\Marble Blast Super\marble\client\ui :

NON-IGNITION VERSION:

ignitionstatusgui.gui.dso

IGNITION VERSION:

ignitionGui.gui.dso
ignitionstatusgui.gui.dso

.\Marble Blast Super\marble\client\scripts :
